# 🚖 GoMotion Rides Dashboard 2025

A Power BI dashboard analyzing ride-hailing operations for GoMotion — covering ride volume, revenue, driver performance, cancellations, and customer satisfaction across 2025.

![Dashboard Preview](GoMotion-Ride-Dashboard.png)

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes 10,000 ride requests on the GoMotion platform to uncover patterns in demand, revenue, and service quality. The goal was to build an interactive Power BI dashboard that lets stakeholders filter by **driver** and **month**, and quickly answer questions like:

- When do customers request rides, and when do they cancel?
- Which locations generate the most revenue?
- Why are rides being cancelled, and can it be reduced?
- Is driver supply keeping up with demand?

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Filters:** Driver ID and Month slicers for granular drill-down
- **Ride Request Trend:** Hourly demand pattern across a 24-hour cycle
- **Revenue by Location:** Top pickup zones ranked by revenue (Oxford Circus, King's Cross, Euston, Camden, Victoria)
- **Cancellation Reasons:** Breakdown of 7 cancellation categories (wrong car, driver rudeness, long wait time, etc.)
- **Time-of-Day Analysis:** Ride requests and cancellations split by Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Night
- **Ride Status Breakdown:** Completed vs. Cancelled vs. No Driver Available

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Afternoon and night** are the busiest periods for both ride requests (63% combined) *and* cancellations (63% combined) — cancellation volume tracks demand volume.
- **Average rating of 2/5** combined with "driver was rude" and "driver asked to cancel" as leading cancellation reasons points to a **driver-side service quality issue**.
- **10% of all requests** go unfulfilled due to no driver being available — a potential **driver supply gap**, especially during peak afternoon/night windows.
- Revenue is **fairly evenly spread** across the top 5 locations (£19.8K–£22.7K), rather than concentrated in one hub.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Investigate driver-level ratings and conduct to address the low average score.
2. Introduce shift incentives to boost driver availability during afternoon/night peaks.
3. Improve driver-vehicle matching and dispatch speed to reduce "wrong car" and "long wait" cancellations.
4. Explore increasing driver density in top-revenue zones (Oxford Circus, King's Cross).
